JEV Autofill

The API key and profile stay in this project's files and are read by the local helper. The JEV modes need TYPESAFE_API_KEY in .env.

In the JEV mode every field is mapped by JEV and every dropdown, radio, button row and tick box is answered by JEV, from your profile or, for a screening question your profile doesn't answer, its best guess from your facts. The safety rules still apply in every mode: nothing is submitted, consent boxes, IDs, bank details and passwords are never filled, and legal and EEO questions are never guessed.

Leave this on unless an agent drives the browser for you: with it off, any click on the toolbar button or the in-page Fill with JEV button fills the page straight away.
